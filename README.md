# Leash Protocol
It's basically a smart safety leash for crypto trading bots and AI agents so they don't go rogue and drain your wallet. Normally, if you give an AI bot access to your crypto, it can accidentally blow past your daily budget, get tricked into a bad trade, or if you put your spending limits publicly on the blockchain, other bots can see your exact strategy and trade against you. With Leash, you keep your actual spending limits completely secret offchain, and every time your bot tries to make a swap, our system uses zero-knowledge proofs to mathematically verify that the trade follows your rules without revealing what those rules actually are—automatically blocking bad trades before you even waste a cent on gas fees, plus giving you a live dashboard with an instant kill switch
